03 · One-week opportunity

Use Higgsfield to build an asset library—not an AI-looking feed.

Yes, create video assets for every carpet. Do not publish one repetitive product Reel after another. First discover two reliable motion treatments, then batch them across the catalog and combine them with real evidence and expert narration.

✓

The target: two approved vertical motion clips for each live carpet, plus extra variations for the five strongest pieces. That creates a useful library of roughly 45–50 final clips from 100+ raw generations.

Non-negotiable rejection test

If the carpet changes, discard the clip.

The border, medallion, colors, dimensions, fringe, pile, wear, and surrounding architecture must not mutate. A beautiful fake detail is still false product information.

No invented rooms No pattern morphing No fake restoration No impossible scale No synthetic owner No fake “historic footage”

Prompt A · Full-carpet revealCopy-ready

Use the uploaded photograph as a strict visual reference. Preserve this exact carpet: identical border, medallion, motifs, colors, fringe, wear, proportions, and texture. The carpet remains perfectly still and flat. Only the camera moves in a very slow, elegant overhead push-in with natural documentary lighting. No morphing, no new details, no restoration, no fabric movement, no scale change, no invented objects. Vertical 9:16, 5–7 seconds, seamless calm ending.

Prompt B · Contextual revealCopy-ready

Animate this exact contextual photograph without redesigning it. Keep the carpet, architecture, street, people, and every visible object faithful to the source image. Add only a restrained cinematic dolly or slow arc that reveals the relationship between the carpet and its place. Documentary realism, stable geometry, natural light, no fantasy elements, no pattern changes, no fake historical action. Vertical 9:16, 5–7 seconds.

06 · Creative

Build Reels for retention and sending.

A luxury account can still move quickly. Use strong questions and reveals without sacrificing taste, evidence, or the real proportions of the carpets.

0–1.5s

Hook

A visual interruption and one concise claim or question.

1.5–5s

Reveal

Show the entire carpet quickly so the promise is paid off.

5–12s

Evidence

Two or three meaningful details, not decorative filler.

12–22s

Fact + action

Origin, age, dimensions, then one SKU or DM action.

Production rules

  • Real carpet, real environment, real voice. No AI-generated rooms.
  • Record vertically in 9:16 and keep the subject inside safe screen margins.
  • Use clean Persian subtitles; assume many viewers start muted.
  • Use restrained music and preserve useful original sounds.
  • One Reel, one idea, one CTA.
  • Always identify the carpet by SKU when inviting an inquiry.
