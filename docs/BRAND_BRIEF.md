# Hosseintalab Brand Brief

## Business transition

Hosseintalab is a multigenerational Persian carpet business based in Tehran's carpet bazaar. Its digital storefront has two primary commercial paths: `فرش برای خانه` for residential buyers throughout Iran and `فرش آنتیک` for specialist buyers seeking rare and collectible pieces.

## Brand challenge

The existing reputation lives primarily in personal relationships and the physical bazaar. The new identity and storefront must convert that reputation into visible, practical trust for customers who may know very little about handwoven carpets.

## Primary audiences

Apartment owners and couples furnishing or redesigning a contemporary home who want an authentic handwoven carpet but feel uncertain about quality, price, size, color, and online purchasing risk.

Collectors, experienced buyers, and interior designers seeking an antique carpet with expert assessment of authenticity, age, condition, and restoration.

## Supporting audiences

- Families seeking a durable and culturally meaningful purchase
- Interior designers and architects sourcing carpets for residential projects

## Customer tension

The customer wants the beauty, durability, and cultural value of a Persian handwoven carpet but fears choosing incorrectly, paying unfairly, receiving something different from the photographs, or buying a product whose authenticity they cannot judge.

## Brand role

Hosseintalab is the trusted expert who makes choosing an authentic handwoven carpet clear, tasteful, and low-risk.

## Core promise

Expert selection and honest guidance for bringing an authentic Persian handwoven carpet into today's home.

## Reasons to believe

- A real family business in Tehran's carpet bazaar
- Multigenerational product expertise
- Experience with rare, antique, and export-quality carpets
- Physical inspection and expert selection of each piece
- Product-specific information, imagery, video, and authenticity records
- A real store, accountable seller, and documented purchasing policies

Claims must be supported by publishable evidence before they appear in customer-facing material.

## Personality

- Expert, not intimidating
- Established, not old-fashioned
- Refined, not ostentatious
- Warm, not overly formal
- Iranian, not reliant on generic cultural clichés
- Contemporary, not trend-dependent

## Verbal principles

- Explain specialist knowledge in ordinary Persian
- Begin with the customer's room, needs, and decision
- Use technical language as evidence, then translate it
- Prefer precise facts over broad quality claims
- Avoid exaggerated luxury language
- Never manufacture history, scarcity, provenance, or investment value

## Visual principles

- A genuine Persian-carpet structural reference should be identifiable
- Heritage should come through proportion, detail, and material sensitivity
- The system must work in contemporary digital retail
- Ornament must remain subordinate to recognition and usability
- Navy, antique gold, and warm ivory are the provisional core palette

## Current naming direction

- Primary Latin name: `HOSSEINTALAB`
- Persian name: `حسین‌طلب`
- Historical business name: `صادرات فرش حسین‌طلب و پسران`
- `& SONS` remains under consideration for a heritage lockup, not necessarily the consumer master logo

## Positioning territory

The preferred territory combines three ideas:

1. Expertise from Tehran's carpet bazaar
2. Clear guidance for a non-specialist buyer
3. Authentic handwoven carpets selected for contemporary homes
