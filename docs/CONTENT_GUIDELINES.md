# Persian Content and Brand Voice Guidelines

## Purpose

Hosseintalab should sound like an experienced carpet seller who respects the customer: knowledgeable, warm, direct, and never condescending.

## Voice

- **Expert:** Use correct carpet terminology and concrete facts.
- **Clear:** Explain what a term means for the customer's home.
- **Warm:** Write as a helpful person, not an institution.
- **Confident:** Recommend without exaggeration or pressure.
- **Iranian:** Use natural contemporary Persian, not translated syntax.

## Writing rules

### Prefer

- Short sentences with a clear subject and verb
- Everyday Iranian Persian
- Direct verbs such as ببینید، بفرستید، مقایسه کنید and انتخاب کنید
- Concrete information: dimensions, material, origin, pattern, condition
- Customer context: room, furniture, color, budget, delivery
- «شما» when direct address improves clarity

### Avoid

- Word-for-word translations from English
- Stacked abstract nouns
- Empty luxury terms such as خاص، بی‌نظیر، نفیس and لاکچری without evidence
- Museum-like language for everyday products
- Claims about investment value
- Unverified age, provenance, dye, knot, workshop, or founding year
- Phrases that make generated reference imagery sound like real inventory

## Examples

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| انتخاب‌هایی واقعی و متناسب | چند گزینه‌ی مناسب |
| فرش در خانه زندگی کند | فرش در فضای واقعی خانه دیده شود |
| انتخاب‌ها را محدود می‌کنیم | چند فرش مناسب پیشنهاد می‌کنیم |
| شخصیت بصری نقشه | ظاهر و ساختار نقشه |
| بودجه‌ی راحت شما | حدود بودجه |
| شناختی که به کار شما می‌آید | تجربه‌ای که موقع خرید به کارتان می‌آید |
| برای کدام فضا می‌گردید؟ | فرش را برای کدام فضا می‌خواهید؟ |

## Product naming

Use a useful, memorable name supported by factual attributes.

Recommended pattern:

`[origin or pattern] + [distinctive field color or feature]`

Examples:

- ماهی تبریز، زمینه‌ی سرمه‌ای
- هریس اناری
- نائین روشن

Do not imply rarity, age, or provenance unless documented.

## Product-description order

1. What the customer sees
2. Exact size
3. Weaving origin
4. Pattern
5. Pile, foundation, and technical details
6. Condition and repairs
7. Suitable room or furnishing context
8. Availability and consultation action

## Terminology

- Use `محل بافت` for the origin browsing dimension.
- Use `قواره` with exact dimensions; never treat the market name as exact.
- Use `طرح و نقشه` in customer-facing navigation.
- Keep specialized terms in the product record and explain them when necessary.
- Write `سرمه‌ای` as the preferred spelling.
- Use Persian numerals in customer-facing Persian text.

## Punctuation and typography

- Use Persian comma `،` and semicolon `؛`.
- Use the Persian question mark `؟`.
- Use نیم‌فاصله in forms such as خانه‌ی، فرش‌های، می‌شود and دستباف.
- Avoid unnecessary quotation marks and exclamation marks.
- Headings may omit a final full stop; complete body sentences should include one.

## Calls to action

Calls to action should describe the result:

- فرش‌های موجود را ببینید
- قیمت و اطلاعات بیشتر
- عکس فضایم را می‌فرستم
- درخواست مشاوره
- پیش از مراجعه هماهنگ کنید

Avoid vague labels such as بیشتر، ادامه and کشف کنید.

## Review checklist

- Does the sentence sound natural when read aloud by an Iranian speaker?
- Is the customer told what happens next?
- Is every quality or history claim supported?
- Does the copy distinguish a sample image from an available product?
- Are technical terms accurate and useful?
- Is the tone helpful rather than intimidating?
