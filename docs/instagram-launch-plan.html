05 · Creative structure

Build for retention and sending.

A refined brand can still move quickly. Show the full carpet early, then earn attention with useful evidence—not empty cinematic filler.

0–1.5s

Hook

A visual interruption plus one concise question or claim. No logo animation.

1.5–5s

Reveal

Show the complete carpet quickly so the opening promise is paid off.

5–12s

Evidence

Show two or three meaningful details: construction, pattern, condition or scale.

12–22s

Fact + action

Origin, dimensions, price or condition—then one DM, WhatsApp or link action.

Production rules

  • Persian subtitles on every Reel; assume viewers begin muted.
  • One Reel, one idea and one call to action.
  • Use a real expert voice regularly.
  • Keep the complete carpet recognisable and geometrically faithful.
  • Use the readable carpet name, not only its inventory code.
  • Do not upload another platform's watermark.
  • Disclose realistically generated or materially altered media when required.

08 · Learning loop

09 · Protect the brand

Virality must not damage trust.

Hosseintalab sells one-of-one, high-consideration pieces. False context, stale availability or exaggerated history may create reach while destroying the reason a serious buyer would choose the business.

Reference imagery

Project documentation says several room and contextual images are production references, not final catalogue evidence. Do not present them as documentary photographs of the actual carpet in a real home.

Inventory mismatch

The content library includes 20 SKU sets while the current launch playbook identifies 19 live product pages. Confirm the exact active SKU before publishing any product post.

Generated motion

If the border, medallion, colour, fringe, proportions, wear or scale changes, reject the clip. A beautiful invented detail is still false product information.

History and condition

Use approximate age, provenance, restoration and technical claims only when supported. Keep evidence and expert opinion clearly separated.
