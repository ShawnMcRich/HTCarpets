# Project Roadmap

## Goal

Launch a Persian-first commerce brand that translates Hosseintalab's Tehran Bazaar expertise into a trustworthy, modern buying experience for customers throughout Iran.

## Phase 0 — Project foundation

**Outcome:** The work has clear ownership, priorities, tools, and decision records.

- Establish the Git repository and working conventions
- Create the Jira Kanban board
- Confirm business owner, project owner, and approval process
- Record the brand, catalog, operational, and technical unknowns
- Define launch success measures

**Exit criteria:** Repository, board, decision log, and initial backlog are active.

## Phase 1 — Brand foundation and identity

**Outcome:** A finalized identity that works across digital and physical applications.

- Verify the official business history and founding year
- Confirm the public brand name and supporting descriptors
- Finalize the Persian medallion and `HT` monogram
- Refine the `HOSSEINTALAB` wordmark
- Define primary, horizontal, monogram, and one-color logo variants
- Establish color, typography, spacing, and minimum-size rules
- Define brand voice in Persian
- Test the identity in website, Instagram, packaging, invoice, and carpet-label contexts

**Exit criteria:** Approved vector logo system and a concise brand guide.

## Phase 2 — Commerce and catalog operations

**Outcome:** The business can reliably publish, sell, ship, and support unique carpets.

- Audit launch inventory
- Select the first 30–50 products
- Define category, filter, and collection taxonomy
- Create the product-data and authenticity-certificate schema
- Establish photography and video standards
- Confirm pricing, inventory ownership, and sold-item behavior
- Define payment, shipping, insurance, return, damage, and refund policies
- Define WhatsApp, Instagram, online checkout, and store-visit workflows
- Confirm legal, tax, invoicing, trust-mark, and payment-gateway requirements

**Exit criteria:** A complete sample catalog and approved operational policies exist.

## Phase 3 — Customer experience and content design

**Outcome:** The storefront architecture and content answer the buyer's real questions.

- Map primary customer journeys
- Define site map and navigation
- Wireframe home, collection, product, consultation, heritage, and policy pages
- Design browsing by size, room, color, style, weaving region, and price
- Define product comparison and consultation experiences
- Write the core Persian messaging hierarchy
- Prepare SEO page and content architecture
- Define analytics events and conversion goals

**Exit criteria:** Approved responsive wireframes, content model, and measurement plan.

## Phase 4 — Storefront implementation

**Outcome:** A fast, secure, Persian-first storefront ready for real catalog data.

- Select the commerce platform after operational requirements are confirmed
- Implement RTL design and responsive components
- Build catalog, filters, search, product pages, cart, and checkout
- Integrate payment, inventory, shipping, messaging, and analytics
- Implement product structured data and technical SEO
- Build editorial, FAQ, heritage, consultation, and policy pages
- Add accessibility, performance, security, backup, and error monitoring

**Exit criteria:** Staging storefront passes functional, content, security, and performance review.

## Phase 5 — Catalog production and launch preparation

**Outcome:** The storefront contains a complete, credible launch collection.

- Photograph and film the launch catalog
- Produce product descriptions and technical records
- Prepare authenticity documents
- Load and verify products
- Produce foundational Instagram content
- Train the team on orders, inventory, inquiries, and returns
- Run test purchases and shipping simulations
- Complete cross-device and browser QA

**Exit criteria:** Launch checklist is complete and the business can fulfill a real order end to end.

## Phase 6 — Controlled launch

**Outcome:** The storefront launches safely and produces measurable customer behavior.

- Soft-launch to existing customers and trusted contacts
- Resolve usability and operational issues
- Launch Instagram and search campaigns gradually
- Monitor checkout, inquiries, fulfillment, returns, and site errors
- Collect early customer feedback and approved testimonials

**Exit criteria:** Stable operations and an evidence-based post-launch priority list.

## Phase 7 — Growth

**Outcome:** Improve conversion and expand demand without weakening brand trust.

- Improve collection and product pages using behavioral data
- Expand SEO content and room-based buying guides
- Establish the interior-designer program
- Add customer-home stories and case studies
- Test virtual consultation, home viewing, and payment options
- Expand the `فرش آنتیک` path with verified inventory records, condition reports, and private-viewing workflows
- Introduce international purchasing only when operationally justified

## Recommended delivery order

Brand and operations must converge before development. The website can be prototyped during identity work, but checkout implementation should wait until payment, shipping, return, and catalog workflows are confirmed.
