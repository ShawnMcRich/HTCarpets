# Logo Design Brief

## Status

The circular digital seal is approved for website use. It preserves the supplied Persian-medallion `HT` artwork and adds an arched `HOSSEINTALAB`, `PERSIAN HANDWOVEN CARPETS`, and the verified establishment year 1290 Solar Hijri. Faithful production vectorization of the medallion remains pending.

## Required identity components

1. A distinctive Persian-carpet medallion silhouette
2. An ownable Latin `HT` monogram
3. The exact Latin wordmark `HOSSEINTALAB`
4. A concentric circular seal relationship
5. The descriptor `PERSIAN HANDWOVEN CARPETS`
6. The establishment line `EST. 1290 · SH`

## Design objective

Create a mark that is immediately associated with fine Persian handwoven carpets while remaining credible as a modern Iranian consumer-retail identity. The mark must express specialist heritage without looking like a museum, export bureau, hotel, jewelry company, sports club, or generic luxury label.

## Medallion requirements

- Derive the silhouette from authentic Persian carpet structure
- Use a strong, recognizable outer contour
- Preserve bilateral or radial balance
- Reduce internal ornament to elements that survive reproduction
- Avoid treating random floral decoration as authenticity
- Remain recognizable without color
- Remain visually stable when reduced to an avatar or label

## Monogram requirements

- Use Latin `H` and `T`
- Both letters must be intentional and discoverable
- Do not allow the mark to read primarily as `HH`, `TH`, `IH`, or a trident
- Letterforms and medallion must behave as one composition
- Avoid simply placing unrelated serif initials over a pattern
- Negative spaces must remain open at small sizes

## Wordmark requirements

- Spell `HOSSEINTALAB` exactly
- Use a refined serif or custom lettering compatible with the emblem
- Optically correct kerning across the long name
- Avoid extreme thin strokes that fail on screens or print
- Remain readable in a compact website header
- Confirm font licensing or create sufficiently original custom lettering

## Provisional color direction

- Deep Persian navy
- Muted antique gold
- Warm ivory

The production mark must use flat color values. Metallic gradients may appear in presentation mockups but never define the master artwork.

## Required configurations

- Primary stacked lockup
- Horizontal website lockup
- Monogram or medallion alone
- One-color positive
- One-color reversed
- Small-size simplified version, if required
- Persian supporting lockup after the Persian naming system is finalized

## Reproduction tests

Every finalist must be tested in these conditions:

- 16–32 px favicon or interface icon
- 80–120 px social avatar
- Mobile website header
- Desktop website header
- One-color ink stamp
- Small woven or printed carpet label
- Authenticity certificate
- Invoice and receipt
- Packaging sticker and tape
- Embroidery or foil, if planned
- Dark and light backgrounds

## Rejection criteria

Reject a direction if:

- It relies on color or gradients to remain recognizable
- The HT monogram becomes ambiguous at ordinary viewing size
- The ornament fills in when reduced
- It resembles a generic luxury, jewelry, hotel, or sports identity
- The Persian-carpet reference is only decorative rather than structural
- It cannot be recreated as clean vector geometry
- It introduces an unverified historical claim

## Copy rules within the logo

The approved circular seal contains `HOSSEINTALAB`, `PERSIAN HANDWOVEN CARPETS`, and `EST. 1290 · SH`. The year is verified for publication. `& SONS` and `TEHRAN • IRAN` remain optional supporting-lockup elements and are not part of the current digital seal.

## Final deliverables

- Editable vector master artwork
- SVG, PDF, EPS, and high-resolution PNG exports
- RGB, CMYK, spot-color, and one-color specifications
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules
- Correct and incorrect usage examples
- Type and color specifications
- Stacked, horizontal, monogram, and reversed files
