Before the first generation

The website workflow, button by button.

Names and exact menu positions can change, but the logic stays the same. Begin in the main Video / Image-to-Video generator—not Text-to-Video—and confirm the Generate button is not charging credits.

01

Open Video

Sign in at higgsfield.ai. Open Generate → Video or the Image-to-Video workflow. Avoid Text-to-Video for products.

02

Choose Unlimited

Select the model marked Unlimited in your account. Enhanced Seedance Fast is a good starting point when that is the included model.

03

Upload every reference

Add Full, Context and Detail through normal Reference Image slots. If the product folder has extra angles or macros, add those too. Do not assign Start or End frames.

04

Lock Instagram Reel size

Choose Seedance 2.0, vertical 9:16 and eight to ten seconds. Export full-screen 1080 × 1920 with native audio enabled. Every prompt repeats this lock: no landscape frame, square crop, border, letterbox or pillarbox.

05

Paste one viral prompt

Choose a Disruption, Mystery or Spectacle prompt and copy it in full. Replace @full, @context and @detail with Higgsfield's reference tags if its interface names them differently.

06

Generate three hook takes

Test A: the written opening. Test B: a tighter macro opening. Test C: a complete-carpet scale reveal. Each take is still one finished multi-shot film—not separate clips to assemble.

07

Reject mutations

Compare the first, middle and final frames with all references. Reject changed borders, motifs, inscriptions, portraits, colors, proportions, fringe or wear.

08

Score twice

Use the raw check for visual hook and focus. Then add the real hook text, music and synchronized sound cue and score the finished Reel separately before choosing the winner.

!
The room, atmosphere and people may be invented; the carpet may not be. Use @full for total geometry, @detail for weave evidence and @context for atmosphere. A person can walk beside, point near or carefully handle an edge, but nobody steps on the carpet and no woven figure ever comes alive.

All-viral multi-reference system

Disrupt first. Create a question. Reveal the carpet.

There are no ordinary product-film or commercial prompt categories now. Every direction begins with a strange but physically believable incident and withholds its explanation long enough to earn the next second.

30%Physical disruptionA controlled fall, sudden catch, dropped object, slamming panel or impact begins on frame one without harming the carpet.
30%Unanswered mysteryThe viewer sees something odd before understanding what it has to do with the carpet.
25%Escalation + reversalThe second composition changes the meaning of the opening instead of merely showing another angle.
15%Product payoffThe final composition resolves the question on an exact full view, signature, portrait, motif, weave or scale proof.

Virality-check calibration

A 49/100 raw master can still contain a strong film.

A 94% Hold Rate means the body is keeping attention once the viewer commits. The revised system attacks the remaining bottleneck with a full 1.2-second incident that starts before the viewer understands it.

35
Low Hook Score.Regenerate the first 1.2 seconds with a bizarre physical event already underway. No fade, empty room, distant carpet, slow approach or camera settling.
43
Weak Attention Control.Limit the film to three primary compositions, one realistic person, one physical action and one consistent direction of movement.
56
High Focus Drift.Remove decorative action and extra people. The carpet should fill roughly 60–75% of the frame or be the clearest high-contrast subject.

Score the right stage

Raw visual → finished Reel.

  1. Raw Higgsfield master: now includes its original native score; judge Hook, Visual Cortex, Attention Control, Focus Drift, Auditory Cortex and product accuracy.
  2. Finished Reel: preserve the generated audio, add the bilingual hook at 0:00 and replace music only when the native score genuinely fails.
  3. Run the virality check again; only the finished text-and-audio version can fairly judge Language Network and Overall Score.
  4. Keep the strong 94% hold body and replace only the opening or soundtrack when possible.
!
The bizarre event happens around the carpet—never to it. A person may perform a safe staged stumble, an empty frame may fall, a magnifier may be caught or a panel may slam open. Nothing heavy, wet, sharp, hot or dirty touches the textile; nobody is injured; the carpet is never thrown, struck, stepped on or used as a stunt surface.

Final gate

Beautiful is not enough. It must be true.

Judge at full-screen size. A clip passes only when a buyer could compare it with the product photographs and recognize the same carpet without qualification.

Approve when…

  • The border count and shape remain identical.
  • The central medallion, tree, vase, stripes, or figures do not morph.
  • Colors do not drift between frames.
  • Fringe and edges stay attached and physically plausible.
  • The strange action is safe, readable and clearly separate from the carpet.
  • The output fills a 1080 × 1920 vertical Reel without bars or cropping.

Reject immediately when…

  • Woven animals or people begin moving.
  • Calligraphy changes, shimmers, or becomes invented text.
  • Damage disappears or the pile looks newly restored.
  • Extra flowers, borders, furniture, or buildings appear.
  • The carpet stretches, breathes, ripples, floats, or changes size.
  • A dramatic camera move hides product inaccuracies.
