Before the first generation

The website workflow, button by button.

Names and exact menu positions can change, but the logic stays the same. Begin in the main Video / Image-to-Video generator—not Text-to-Video—and confirm the Generate button is not charging credits.

01

Open Video

Sign in at higgsfield.ai. Open Generate → Video or the Image-to-Video workflow. Avoid Text-to-Video for products.

02

Choose Unlimited

Select the model marked Unlimited in your account. Enhanced Seedance Fast is a good starting point when that is the included model.

03

Check the card type

In the truthful product workflow, Context and Full go to Higgsfield while Detail uses editor keyframes. The separate Viral Campaign kit later uses all three as multi-reference evidence.

04

Set the frame

Choose vertical 9:16, five seconds, and 720p when that is the highest Unlimited setting. Turn generated audio off for clean masters.

05

Paste the prompt

Copy the entire English prompt. If there is a separate motion selector, choose the same restrained motion named on the card.

06

Generate three AI takes

For Context and Full only, run the identical prompt three times. The Detail clip needs no AI generation and therefore no alternate takes.

07

Reject aggressively

Compare the first, middle, and final frames to the source. Reject any changing border, motif, fringe, scale, text, face, or architecture.

08

Download + rename

Keep only the winner. Use the output filename shown on the card, then check the task box and record the model/take in Notes.

!
Do not ask a crop to reveal what is outside it. That is an outpainting request disguised as camera movement, so invented motifs are unavoidable. A Detail card now uses non-generative editor keyframes that move only across pixels already present in the real detail photograph.

01

Install Edits

Use Meta’s free Edits app on your phone, or CapCut if you already know it. Edits has a frame-accurate timeline and exports without an added watermark.

02

Start 9:16

Create a new vertical project. Import approved Shot A, Shot B, Shot C, then the supplied CTA PNG for that SKU—in exactly that order.

03

Trim the clips

A = 3.0s, B = 4.0s, C = 4.0s, CTA card = 3.0s. Total target: 14 seconds. Choose each video clip’s most stable section; never shorten the CTA.

04

Use hard cuts

Place the clips directly against each other. No spinning, flash, zoom, glitch, page-turn, or AI transition. The movement is already inside the shots.

05

Add four text cues

Use the exact Persian + English lines in each Reel kit. Persian is the main line; English is smaller beneath it. Use off-white text and a subtle dark shadow—not a loud template.

06

Add music—no voice needed

Mute every Higgsfield clip. Add one instrumental track at 35–45% volume, align the cuts near 0:03 / 0:07 / 0:11, and let the music continue under the CTA before fading only the final 0.2 seconds.

07

Keep text safe

Keep important text away from the extreme top, bottom, and right edge where Instagram’s interface sits. Preview the Reel full-screen before export.

08

Export one master

Export vertical 1080 × 1920, H.264, 30 fps, highest quality, no watermark. Do not add filters, HDR, artificial sharpening, or color replacement.

The product Reel proves the carpet. The viral campaign Reel earns attention through a question, contradiction, reveal, opinion, or visual challenge. Use both; do not turn the entire page into polished advertisements.

Final gate

Beautiful is not enough. It must be true.

Judge at full-screen size. A clip passes only when a buyer could compare it with the product photographs and recognize the same carpet without qualification.

Approve when…

  • The border count and shape remain identical.
  • The central medallion, tree, vase, stripes, or figures do not morph.
  • Colors do not drift between frames.
  • Fringe and edges stay attached and physically plausible.
  • Any architecture and scale remain stable.
  • The movement is subtle enough to feel intentional.

Reject immediately when…

  • Woven animals or people begin moving.
  • Calligraphy changes, shimmers, or becomes invented text.
  • Damage disappears or the pile looks newly restored.
  • Extra flowers, borders, furniture, or buildings appear.
  • The carpet stretches, breathes, ripples, floats, or changes size.
  • A dramatic camera move hides product inaccuracies.
