# Changelog

All notable changes to the Hosseintalab storefront and supporting product system are recorded here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Persian-first responsive storefront prototype
- Room-based discovery for reception room, living room, bedroom, and small spaces
- Carpet taxonomy by size, weaving origin, and pattern
- Representative imagery for customer-facing categories
- Category-preview dialog with keyboard and mobile behavior
- Featured product presentation with factual product attributes
- Consultation and store-visit conversion paths
- Persistent desktop header and full-screen mobile navigation
- Conversion-focused footer and trust information
- Photography and visual-production direction
- Persian content and brand-voice guidelines
- Project brief, UX decision record, work journal, ADRs, and portfolio draft
- Two primary storefront paths: `فرش برای خانه` and `فرش آنتیک`
- Dedicated antique-carpet introduction, expertise criteria, and consultation path
- Approved circular digital seal in portable SVG and high-resolution PNG formats
- Responsive logo family: light and reversed horizontal lockups, stacked lockup, favicons, app icons, social avatars, and watermark
- Room selector with immediate visual and practical guidance
- Keyboard-operable catalog tabs and accessible category previews
- Skip navigation, visible focus treatment, reduced-motion support, and responsive touch targets

### Changed

- Redirected the visual direction from a mysterious gallery toward warm residential commerce
- Replaced generic carpet categories with actual market terminology
- Corrected visual-category mismatches
- Reworked header and footer structure and functionality
- Rewrote all customer-facing Persian copy in natural contemporary Persian
- Re-exported the medallion asset with complete lower-point rendering and safe padding
- Replaced the former header, menu, and footer lockups with responsive uses of the circular seal
- Updated navigation, metadata, brand copy, and heritage messaging for the two-part product architecture
- Reorganized the storefront into a shorter residential buying journey with one clear discovery sequence
- Assigned purpose-specific logo variants to desktop header, mobile header, menu, heritage section, and footer
- Reworked hero hierarchy, trust signals, product inquiry flow, consultation block, and responsive layouts

### Fixed

- Category items no longer jump to the featured collection
- Category images now open the correct preview
- Missing or mismatched category artwork
- Cropped lower section of the medallion logo
- Mobile-menu focus, Escape, and background-scroll behavior
- Internal header and footer navigation targets
- Nonfunctional save controls, self-links, and category links that did not change page state
- Mixed Latin/Persian numerals in interface sequence labels
